# ISO 32212 Net-Zero Transition Planning Applicability and Control Plan

## 1. Applicability decision first

ISO 32212:2026 addresses net-zero transition planning for financial institutions. Cito/CPay must not claim the standard applies, or claim conformity, merely because CPay provides payment services.

The legal/compliance owner must record one of these decisions:

- `APPLICABLE`: the responsible legal entity is within the intended financial-institution scope and the organization adopts ISO 32212 as a formal control framework;
- `VOLUNTARY`: the organization is not required to apply it but chooses to adopt it;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: documented legal/business analysis concludes it is outside scope.

The decision, approver, date, legal entity and evidence reference belong in the controlled governance/evidence system and are reviewed after material entity, licence or product changes.

## 2. Transition-plan governance when applicable/voluntary

The transition plan must have board/executive sponsorship and an accountable owner independent enough to challenge unsupported sustainability claims. Responsibilities cover target setting, data, technology/infrastructure, procurement/suppliers, finance, risk, compliance and communications.

## 3. Baseline and boundaries

The plan identifies the organizational and operational boundary used for emissions and transition metrics, including material cloud/infrastructure, offices, business travel and supplier categories. If financed emissions or other financial-sector portfolio metrics are relevant to the legal/business model, the methodology and data limitations are explicitly documented rather than inferred from payment throughput.

## 4. Objectives and targets

Targets are measurable, time-bound and linked to a defined baseline/methodology. Each target includes:

- baseline year and metric;
- organizational/operational boundary;
- target year and interim milestones;
- responsible owner;
- actions/investments;
- assumptions and dependencies;
- data-quality limitations;
- measurement/evidence source;
- review and corrective-action process.

Targets must distinguish actual emissions reduction from offsets/credits and state the treatment of each.

## 5. Integration with Cito management systems

Climate-transition matters are integrated into:

- ISO 9001 context, interested parties and relevant objectives;
- ISO/IEC 27001 supplier/cloud risks when transition decisions affect availability/security;
- ISO/IEC 20000 service capacity/supplier decisions;
- ISO 22301 climate/environmental continuity scenarios;
- procurement and supplier evaluation;
- enterprise risk and management review.

A carbon-reduction change is not permitted to weaken security, financial integrity, recoverability or legal compliance without explicit risk treatment and approval.

## 6. Technology and supplier controls

Where material, service and supplier reviews capture energy/emissions-related evidence available from cloud/data-centre/telecommunications providers while avoiding unverifiable estimates. Architecture decisions may consider efficiency alongside security, reliability, latency, residency, cost and continuity.

Critical supplier sustainability claims used in Cito disclosures require a traceable source and period.

## 7. Data quality and anti-greenwashing control

External sustainability statements must be supported by defined data, assumptions and approval. Marketing is prohibited from presenting aspirational plans as achieved outcomes or presenting repository configuration as independently assured emissions performance.

Known data gaps, estimation methods and material uncertainty are disclosed to the approving management body.

## 8. Monitoring and management review

When applicable/voluntary, management review includes progress against transition milestones, data quality, supplier developments, climate-related operational/continuity risks, material plan changes and corrective actions.

The internal-audit programme periodically samples the evidence trail supporting material transition-plan claims.

## 9. Current repository posture

The repository establishes governance and applicability controls. It does not contain sufficient verified corporate emissions, supplier-emissions, legal-entity or portfolio data to mark ISO 32212 conformity complete. Those are external/manual evidence requirements and remain fail-closed until authorized evidence is recorded.
