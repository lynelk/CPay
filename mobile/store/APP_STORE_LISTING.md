## Description

Cito Business gives merchants secure mobile access to the services they use on the Cito platform.

Understand today's collections and payouts, search transactions, review payment-channel readiness, inspect transaction timelines, receive operational notifications and contact support without returning to a desktop.

Cito is more than payments. Depending on your approved account entitlements and configured providers, the platform can also support communications, identity and business verification, credit intelligence and scoring, vending and value-added services, billing and monetisation, APIs, webhooks and automation.

Key capabilities:

- Secure merchant sign-in and session restoration
- Live collection, payout and transaction summaries
- Transaction search and canonical processing timelines
- Guarded mobile-money collection initiation
- Ledger-derived merchant balance and recent statement entries
- Payment-channel readiness and production-limit context
- Cito service and entitlement overview
- Notifications and merchant support cases
- In-app account-deletion request

Availability of individual channels and services depends on merchant approval, country, provider configuration, certification and entitlement. Cito does not represent a provider as live merely because an integration adapter exists.

## Review notes

Cito Business is an authenticated merchant application. Provide App Review with a dedicated sandbox merchant account containing non-sensitive test transactions and service entitlements. Include the merchant account number, username and password in the private review credentials field.

The review account must not have production disbursement authority. A sandbox collection can be initiated from Payments → Receive and reviewed in the transaction timeline.

Account deletion is initiated from More → Settings & security → Request account deletion.
