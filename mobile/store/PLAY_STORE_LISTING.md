Cito Business

## Short description

Payments, balances, services and support for Cito merchants.

## Full description

Cito Business is the secure mobile workspace for merchants using the Cito platform.

Track collections and payouts, search transaction references, review payment-channel readiness, see ledger-derived account information, receive notifications and create contextual support cases from your phone or tablet.

Cito also brings other approved business services into one account. Depending on merchant entitlement and provider readiness, these can include SMS, WhatsApp Business and USSD communications; NIN, KYC and KYB verification; CRB reports and credit scoring; airtime, data and utility vending; billing, metering, rating and invoicing; APIs, webhooks and automation.

Features include:

- Secure merchant authentication
- Collection and payout summaries
- Transaction search and status evidence
- Guarded collection requests
- Balances and statement entries
- Payment-channel status
- Service and entitlement catalogue
- Notifications
- Support and account-deletion requests

Cito shows only live data returned by the platform. It does not invent balances, provider states or transaction figures when information is unavailable.

Service and provider availability varies by country, commercial approval, configuration, certification and merchant entitlement.
